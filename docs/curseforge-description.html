ComradeCraft: profession leveling for WoW Forever, built for groups of friends

Stop guessing who needs what. ComradeCraft plans the cheapest (or most profitable) way to level every crafting profession on WoW Forever, from the materials in your bags and the auction house prices, and shares it with your friends so the whole group levels faster and spends less gold.

Sound familiar?

  • You sell Light Leather on the auction house, and your friend buys it back an hour later.
  • Your leveling guide says "craft 20 of X", but you already have materials for something cheaper.
  • You craft hundreds of items that sell for less than their materials.
  • You leave the game to look up where a recipe is taught or which quest unlocks Artisan.
  • Your friend logs off before you can ask them to craft something for you.
  • You forget profession cooldowns on your alts.

ComradeCraft fixes each of these, in game, with no website and no spreadsheet.

Features

  • Group material tooltip: hover any item to see who in your group needs it, how many, and how soon.
  • Personal leveling route: recipes to learn, intermediates to craft, skill-up chance of each step, and total cost, computed from your bags, your known recipes and live prices.
  • Cheapest or Profitable route: Profitable counts what each product brings back (merchant, disenchant or auction house, after the 5% cut and the deposit). Cost and net cost for every 75-point milestone.
  • Step panel and craft queue next to the profession window: current step, skill-up chance, crafts your bags allow, and a "Craft next" button.
  • Shopping lists: export missing materials to Auctionator, or copy a search text and group import for TradeSkillMaster.
  • Where to learn a recipe: trainers, vendors and drops, with a TomTom waypoint to the nearest one.
  • Next rank guidance: the Expert book of Cooking, First Aid and Fishing, the Artisan quest (with Questie), or the trainer.
  • Craft requests: ask a friend to craft a recipe for you. The request waits until you are both online.
  • Mail helper: attaches what a group member needs. You still press Send.
  • Disenchant or keep: advice for the items in your bags, based on group needs and prices.
  • Alerts, each one can be turned off: materials ready, cooldowns ready on any of your characters, new craft requests, and (opt-in) upgrades a friend can craft for you.

Get started in 3 steps

  1. Type /comrade to open the window. A short tour shows the basics.
  2. In Manage group > Your characters, check the characters you want to share.
  3. Add your friends with /comrade add Name (both players add each other).

Playing alone? The leveling route, step panel, shopping lists and alerts work without a group.

Works with your addons

Nothing else is required. ComradeCraft detects these optional addons:

  • TradeSkillMaster or Auctionator: auction prices, disenchant values and shopping lists (merchant prices otherwise).
  • Questie: positions of trainers, vendors and quest givers.
  • TomTom: waypoints.
  • Pawn: upgrade detection with each friend's stat scale (item level otherwise).

FAQ

Which game version? WoW Forever, client 1.60.1 (Interface 16001).

Which professions? Alchemy, Blacksmithing, Cooking, Enchanting, Engineering, First Aid, Leatherworking and Tailoring get leveling routes. Fishing, Mining, Herbalism and Skinning are tracked for group needs.

Do my friends need the addon? Yes, to share their needs and receive craft requests. Your own route works without them.

Is my data sent to anyone? No character data leaves your game until you share a character, and then only by addon whisper to the players you added who added you back. At login, a presence signal without character data goes to your guild's addon channel so guildmates can find each other; /comrade guildping off turns it off.

Where do the skill-up chances come from? The Classic formula, calibrated with the crafts you and your group log.

Languages? English and French, following your game language.

Commands

  • /comrade: open the group window
  • /comrade shop [profession]: list and export the materials to buy
  • /comrade tracker [reset]: show or hide the step panel
  • /comrade mail Name: at a mailbox, attach what Name needs
  • /comrade share [off]: share this character with your group
  • /comrade add Name / remove Name: add or remove a player

Every command also has a button in the window.

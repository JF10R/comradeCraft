ComradeCraft: profession leveling for WoW Forever, built for groups of friends

Stop guessing who needs what. ComradeCraft plans the cheapest (or most profitable) way to level every crafting profession on WoW Forever, from the materials in your bags and the auction house prices, and shares it with your friends so the whole group levels faster and spends less gold.

Sound familiar?

  • You sell Light Leather on the auction house, and your friend buys it back an hour later.
  • Your leveling guide says "craft 20 of X", but you already have materials for something cheaper.
  • You craft hundreds of items that sell for less than their materials.
  • You leave the game to look up where a recipe is taught or which quest unlocks Artisan.
  • Your friend logs off before you can ask them to craft something for you.
  • You forget profession cooldowns on your alts.

ComradeCraft fixes each of these, in game, with no website and no spreadsheet. Everything is one click away from its minimap button.

Features

  • Group plan & synergies: by default, routes spend the least gold for the whole group (materials a member already holds count as free). One view shows what the group still needs, who holds spare stock, cross-profession synergies, and what is left to buy.
  • Shared craft wishlist: pin any craft for yourself or a friend (bags, gear, enchants), track its materials in a movable on-screen tracker, and get alerted when it is ready to craft.
  • Farming assignments & Buy vs Farm advice: opt-in farming tracker for group needs plus gold-aware Buy vs Farm recommendations with QuestieDB farm spots and TomTom waypoints.
  • Bag highlights & tooltips: highlights group-needed items directly in Blizzard bags, Baganator, Bagnon, Combuctor and ElvUI, focusing on the active mailbox or trade recipient. Hover any material or recipe item to see who needs it and how soon.
  • Keep for the group: at a merchant or the auction house, the items in your bags that your group needs. Sell one anyway and a "Buy back" button appears.
  • Personal leveling route: recipes to learn, intermediates to craft, skill-up chance of each step, and total cost, recalculated automatically from your bags, newly learned recipes (world drops, vendors, Forever custom recipes) and live prices.
  • Cheapest or Profitable route: Profitable counts what each product brings back (merchant, disenchant or auction house, after the 5% cut and the deposit). Cost and net cost for every 75-point milestone.
  • Step panel and craft queue next to the profession window: current step, skill-up chance, crafts your bags allow, and a "Craft next" button.

  • Shopping, Merchant & Auction House panels: export missing materials to Auctionator or TSM, auto-buy vendor reagents in one click at merchants, and search missing materials directly at the Auction House with your gold balance in view.
  • Where to learn a recipe: trainers, vendors and drops, with a TomTom waypoint to the nearest one. Trainer visits are remembered and refused recipes are deferred automatically.
  • Next rank guidance: the Expert book of Cooking, First Aid and Fishing, the Artisan quest (with Questie), or the trainer.
  • Craft requests: ask a friend to craft a recipe for you. The request waits until you are both online.
  • Mail & Trade helpers: compact side panels at the Mailbox and Trade window to attach or place what a group member needs in one click. You still press Send or Accept.
  • Guild directory (opt-in, per character): search who in your guild can craft an item and whisper them. Item tooltips name up to 3 guild crafters.
  • Group upgrades audit & Disenchant or keep: spot small bags, missing enchants and craftable gear upgrades across the group, plus BoP-safe disenchant-or-keep advice for your bags.
  • Loot hints & Alerts, each one can be turned off: group loot roll notes, materials ready, wishlist ready, cooldowns ready on any of your characters, new craft requests, and (opt-in) upgrades a friend can craft for you.

Get started in 3 clicks

  1. Click the ComradeCraft minimap button. A short tour shows the basics. Hover the button for a summary of your group.
  2. In Manage group, check the characters you want to share.
  3. Add your friends by name, or accept the guildmates the addon suggests. Both players add each other.

Open a profession window and the step panel appears next to it. Hover any material for the group's needs. Playing alone? The leveling route, step panel, shopping lists and alerts work without a group.

Works with your addons

Nothing else is required. ComradeCraft detects these optional addons:

  • TradeSkillMaster or Auctionator: auction prices, disenchant values and shopping lists (merchant prices otherwise).
  • Questie: positions of trainers, vendors and quest givers.
  • TomTom: waypoints.
  • Pawn: upgrade detection with each friend's stat scale (item level otherwise).
  • Baganator, Bagnon, Combuctor, ElvUI: bag badges and mail/trade highlights (Blizzard bags supported natively).

FAQ

Which game version? WoW Forever, client 1.60.1 (Interface 16001).

Which professions? Alchemy, Blacksmithing, Cooking, Enchanting, Engineering, First Aid, Leatherworking and Tailoring get leveling routes. Fishing, Mining, Herbalism and Skinning are tracked for group needs.

Do my friends need the addon? Yes, to share their needs, farm for the group and receive craft requests. Your own route works without them.

Is my data sent to anyone? No character data leaves your game until you share a character, and then only by addon whisper to the players you added who added you back. At login, a presence signal without character data goes to your guild's addon channel so guildmates can find each other; /comrade guildping off turns it off. A character you list in the guild directory sends its professions and known recipes (nothing else) to your guild's addon channel.

Where do the skill-up chances come from? The Classic formula, calibrated with the crafts you and your group log.

Languages? English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese and Russian, following your game language. Translation fixes are welcome.

Prefer typing? Every button also has a chat command: type /comrade help for the list.

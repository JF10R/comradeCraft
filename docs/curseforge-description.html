The Co-Op Profession & Craftable BiS Brain for WoW Forever

Stop vendoring mats your friends are about to buy. Stop alt-tabbing for static guides.
ComradeCraft turns your alts, friends, and guild into one live crafting economy. 100% in-game, zero spreadsheets, zero external servers (and works great solo too).

Why ComradeCraft?

Static guides don't know what's in your bags. Economy addons don't coordinate your friends. ComradeCraft bridges both: every route, bag highlight, tooltip, bank withdraw, and mail attachment thinks across your entire group.

  • Stop wasting gold: No more selling Silk Cloth on the AH while your friend buys it an hour later. Bags light up, merchants warn you, and Mail, Trade, and Bank helpers move mats in 1 click.
  • Beat static leveling guides: Live Cheapest or Profitable routes adapt to what your group already owns, live AH prices, and automatic 3x Lesser <-> 1x Greater essence conversions.
  • Never alt-tab again: Built-in Expert (150 to 225) & Artisan (225 to 300) trainer and book guide for all 12 professions (Alliance & Horde) plus a Standalone Craftable BiS Engine for all 9 classes and 29 specs.
  • Play at different hours: 100% in-game Mesh Sync relays updates through mutual friends, Guild Sync follows alt switches, and Shared Bank Alts let anyone request mats directly through the addon.

Key Features

Dynamic Leveling & Economy

  • Cheapest or Profitable routes: Live cost per 75-point milestone counting vendor sell, disenchant value, and AH resale (after 5% cut & deposit risk).
  • Automatic Essence Conversions: Seamless 3x Lesser <-> 1x Greater tracking across plans, surplus, mail, bags, and right-click hints in the Tracker & Wishlist.
  • All 12 Expert & Artisan Trainers: Alliance & Horde trainers, rank-unlock books (Cooking, First Aid, Fishing), and Artisan quests (including Mining, Herbalism, Skinning) with QuestieDB + 1-click TomTom waypoints.
  • 1-Click Shopping Lists: Direct Auctionator export or copy-ready TSM exact search strings & groups.

Co-Op Group Brain & Shared Bank Alts

  • Mesh Sync & Guild Channel Sync: Mutually trusted peers automatically relay up-to-date snapshots for offline friends, with zero external apps required.
  • Shared Bank Alts: Flag any alt as a Shared bank alt so 100% of its bags, bank, and mail show as available group stock, searchable with 1-click item requests.
  • Item Reservations (Alt+Right-Click): Lock and tag bag items for a specific friend with a violet lock badge, anti-sell protection, 1-click Bank & Mail queuing, and live sync so they see "Reserved for you" before you even mail it.
  • Bag Highlights, Native Auto-Sort & Tooltips: Crisp colored borders & mailbox badges light up spare mats, reserved items, unknown non-BoP patterns, and request reagents in your bags (focusing in gold on your active Mail or Trade target), plus native ComradeCraft categories in Baganator and BetterBags.
  • Craft Requests, Wishlist & Farming Banner: Request crafts or bank items, track pinned goals on screen, see a live "Farming for you" banner on Group -> Overview, and share missing mats to /g or /p in 1 click.
  • Favorites, Guild Directory & Loot Hints: Star favorite players and preferred crafters to pin them at the top of the Sidebar & Guild Directory (and prioritize them in Upgrades), quick-filter large rosters, search /comrade directory, and get live roll hints on drops.

Standalone Craftable BiS Engine & Patterns to Hunt (9 Classes, 29 Specs)

  • Automatic spec detection from talents & spells (with 1-click UI / /comrade spec override), with zero external addon needed.
  • Top 5 craftable upgrades per slot across yourself (incl. BoP), alts, friends, and guild directory, including 2H vs 1H+OH math, wands, 180+ combat enchants, upcoming +1-4 lvl BiS, and spec consumables.
  • Dedicated Patterns Tab: Lists valuable unlearned gear, enchant formulas, bags, and consumables for yourself and your group, showing where to buy or farm, drop rates (358 Classic recipes + optional Forever Drops/Lootified connector), BoE AH prices, 1-click TomTom waypoints, and 1-click BoE pattern AH search.

Companion Panels Everywhere You Click

  • Mailbox (Blizzard, Postbox, Postal, ElvUI, TSM): 1-click attach for mats, patterns & requests, bank-only clarity, and WoW Forever First+Last name protection.
  • Bank: 1-click Withdraw needed for your current step, Wishlist, or queued group mail.
  • Merchant: Highlights plan reagents and learnable rank books/recipes directly in Blizzard's vendor window, plus a side panel to buy exact missing quantities or buried Page 3 books (like Expert Cookbook for a friend) in 1 click.
  • Auction House: 1-click search for missing mats and BoE BiS patterns with your gold balance in view.
  • Trainer & Trade: Trainer BiS/plan highlighter and 1-click trade fill.
  • Step Panel & Craft Queue: Docks beside your profession window with a Craft next button.

Get Started in 30 Seconds

  1. Click the ComradeCraft minimap button (or type /comrade).
  2. In Manage group, check the characters you want to share (and optionally mark a Shared bank alt).
  3. Add friends by name or click Add on guildmates/party members. Playing solo? Every route, BiS audit, shopping list, and companion panel works out of the box.
